WarFit

A serious workout tracker for serious athletes. 28+ battle-tested training programmes, full logging, smart insights, and a complete toolbox — built by someone who actually trains.

← All projects

Features

📋

28+ Programmes

Military selection prep (JTF-2, Navy SEAL PAST), powerlifting, marathon, 5K sub-20, kettlebell, CrossFit/MetCon, swimming, cycling, triathlon, rucking, and more.

🏆

PR Detection

Automatically flags new personal records the moment you log a heavier lift. No setup needed — it just knows.

📊

Performance Dashboard

Lift progression charts, run time trends, body weight tracking, activity heatmap, and top PR leaderboard all in one view.

📋

Weekly Check-Ins

Rate energy, sleep, soreness, motivation, and stress each week. Smart suggestions adapt based on your wellness patterns.

🔨

Plan Builder

Build completely custom programmes — set your own days, exercises, sets, reps, and notes. Fully integrated into the tracker.

📸

Progress Photos

Log front, side, and back photos over time. Side-by-side before/after comparison with weight change overlay.

⏱️

Session Tools

Built-in rest timer, barbell plate calculator, 1RM estimator, and run pace calculator — all accessible mid-workout.

📐

Body Measurements

Log chest, waist, hips, arms, thighs, and calves over time. Week-over-week delta shown automatically.

🔁

Plan Runs & Archives

Finish a programme? Archive it and restart with a clean slate. Your full history — lifts, runs, notes — is preserved forever.
