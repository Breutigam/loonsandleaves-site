About the name

Loons & Leaves

The loon is Canada's most iconic bird — calm, purposeful, and powerful beneath the surface. The leaf is a nod to the natural world and the cycles of growth. Together, they represent who we are: grounded, intentional, and always building toward something better.

We're an indie studio, which means every line of code and every design decision comes from people who genuinely care about the end result.
