01 Overview

WARFIT ("we," "our," or "us") is a military and tactical fitness tracker developed by Loons & Leaves for Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with care and transparency.

This Privacy Policy explains what information WARFIT collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By downloading and using WARFIT, you agree to the practices described here.

Privacy-first design. WARFIT stores your data locally on your device. The app does not require an account to use any core feature.

02 Information We Collect

WARFIT collects only the minimum data necessary to provide you with a functional fitness tracking experience.

Information you provide directly

  • Workout logs including exercises, sets, reps, and weights
  • Personal fitness goals and training program selections
  • Optional display name or preferences set within the app

Information collected automatically

  • App crash reports and error logs (used solely for debugging)
  • Basic device info such as OS version and screen size (for compatibility)
  • Anonymous app usage analytics, only if you opt in

Information we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, or contact details — no account is required
  • Location or GPS data
  • Camera, microphone, or media library access
  • Payment or financial information
  • Health records beyond the workout data you manually enter

03 How We Use Your Information

We use information collected for the following purposes only:

  • To provide core app functionality — tracking workouts, displaying training plans, and calculating progressive overload suggestions
  • To remember your preferences and workout history across sessions
  • To identify and resolve technical issues through crash and error reporting
  • To improve the app based on aggregated, anonymized usage patterns (only with your consent)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app's fitness functionality.

04 Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

We may share limited, anonymized data with the following service providers only as necessary to operate the app:

  • Crash reporting services — to diagnose and resolve technical issues
  • Analytics providers — only aggregate, non-identifiable usage data, and only if you have opted in

Any third-party providers are contractually bound to use your data only for the specific purpose of providing services to WARFIT and are prohibited from using it for their own ends.

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Loons & Leaves or WARFIT users.

05 Data Storage & Security

Your workout data and app preferences are stored locally on your Android device. We do not operate servers that store your personal fitness data in the cloud.

Where data is transmitted — such as for crash reporting — we use industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS). No method of electronic transmission is 100% secure; we encourage you to use a device passcode to protect your device.

Note: Because data is stored locally, uninstalling WARFIT will permanently remove all workout history and settings from your device.

06 Data Retention

Your locally-stored data is retained on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data through your Android device settings.

Crash reports and anonymized analytics data, if collected, are retained for a maximum of 12 months and then permanently deleted.

07 Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access & portability — your workout data is always accessible to you within the app and on your device
  • Deletion — delete individual entries within the app, or remove all app data via Android Settings → Apps → WARFIT → Clear Data
  • Analytics opt-out — opt out of anonymous usage analytics at any time via the app's Settings menu
  • Correction — edit or correct any workout entries directly within the app

If you are a resident of the EEA, California, or another jurisdiction with specific privacy rights, you may have additional rights under applicable law. Please contact us to exercise these rights.

08 Children's Privacy

WARFIT is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, app features, or applicable law. When material changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide an in-app notification.

Your continued use of WARFIT after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
